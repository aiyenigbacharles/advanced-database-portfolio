Q2: Find orders that have been PENDING for more than 2 days.

SELECT order_id, customer_id, order_date, total_amount
FROM orders
WHERE status = 'PENDING'
  AND order_date < TIMESTAMPTZ '2026-10-15' - INTERVAL '2 days'
ORDER BY order_date;
